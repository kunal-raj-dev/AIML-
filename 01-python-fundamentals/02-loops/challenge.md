# Challenge: Factor Check and FizzBuzz Range

## Requirements
- Take starting and ending integer inputs from the user.
- Loop from start to end (inclusive).
- Print numbers but replace multiples of 3 with 'Fizz', multiples of 5 with 'Buzz', and multiples of both with 'FizzBuzz'.

## Restrictions
- ❌ No AI assistants
- ❌ No online tutorials

## Time Limit
- ⏱️ 30 minutes
