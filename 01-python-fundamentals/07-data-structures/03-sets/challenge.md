# Challenge: Set-Based Unique Word Intersector

## Requirements
- Take two sentences as string inputs.
- Convert each sentence to a set of lowercase words (ignoring punctuation).
- Find and print the shared words (intersection) and unique words (symmetric difference).

## Restrictions
- ❌ No AI assistants
- ❌ No online tutorials

## Time Limit
- ⏱️ 35 minutes
